## Score-default problems found

- The prior run awarded internal-tech score 20 on **150/151** records and outsourcing-fit score 80 on **150/151**. That treated missing tech evidence like known low internal capability.
- The revised model stores `KNOWN_LOW_INTERNAL_TECH` separately from `UNKNOWN_INTERNAL_TECH`; UNKNOWN maps to neutral 50/50 values and lowers confidence rather than awarding a capability-gap bonus.
- The prior score-confidence field used fractional values below 1 on **151/151** records even though the requested field is 0–100. The audit rewrites it to an integer 0–100 scale.
- Prior digital-maturity and component values were often tied because they were derived from defaults. The audit uses observed signal density where available and keeps unknown dimensions neutral.

## Workflow-template problems found

- Prior workflow templates were distributed from industry/category context without requiring a company-specific operating signal. The new run retains a workflow only when public company-page signals support it; otherwise the value is `UNSUPPORTED`.
- Existing workflow-name distribution in the audited cohort: {'Parts knowledge, cross-reference and quotation': 11, 'Order intake and inventory exception workflow': 24, 'Shipment document intake and exception queue': 34, 'Production and quality reporting assistant': 18, 'RFQ-to-quotation and SKU matching': 10, 'Service-ticket and maintenance knowledge assistant': 10, 'Tender, BOQ and contract-obligation search': 13, 'BOQ/specification-to-product matching': 5, 'Technical tender and product-dossier assistant': 6, 'Job brief, estimate and approval workflow': 15, 'Operations reporting and internal knowledge discovery': 1, 'Controlled regulatory and SOP knowledge workflow': 4}.
- Warehouse rental, branch, retail and software-product records are not allowed to inherit freight, manufacturing or generic RFQ workflows from category labels.

## Evidence weaknesses

- **84/151** audited records still have only one independent source family; these cannot pass the READY gate.
- Source-family mix: {'Book-e Avval only': 84, 'Book-e Avval plus corroboration': 67}. Book-e Avval appears in 151/151 records; it was a major discovery input and was not treated as authoritative.
- Targeted audit fetches: 950 public URL tasks; 505 HTTP-success pages and 445 retained failures/timeouts. Every company had at least one usable page under the audit matching rule, but many usable pages are still self-published or directory evidence.
- Public evidence generally cannot establish budget, current workflow volume, decision authority or the absence of an undisclosed internal engineering team; those fields remain UNKNOWN.

## Audit interpretation

The original ranking was materially biased toward directory/category signals and fixed defaults. The corrected ranking is intentionally smaller: only records with corroborated activity and a supported workflow hypothesis are sales-ready. The strongest remaining uncertainty is not a missing score—it is the lack of public proof of internal process volume and buying authority.
