# Evidence policy

- `OBSERVED`: directly stated, returned, or visibly shown by a public source.
- `STRONG_INFERENCE`: supported by multiple observations but not directly stated.
- `WEAK_INFERENCE`: plausible hypothesis requiring discovery validation.
- `UNKNOWN`: not established publicly; never silently converted to false or true.

Every claim stores source URL, title, retrieval time, source quality, confidence and a short quote/snippet. Proposals may state observations as facts. Inferences must be introduced as hypotheses and followed by discovery questions. Industry stereotypes are never company facts.

Only public corporate contact information is collected. No private personal phones, non-public emails, authenticated portals, CAPTCHA bypasses or synthetic screenshots are allowed.
